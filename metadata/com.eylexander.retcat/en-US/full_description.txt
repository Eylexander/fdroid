Where is my bus? Re-TCAT shows the buses of TCAT, the Troyes Champagne Métropole (France) network, in real time. The app is in French.

🗺️ EVERY BUS ON THE MAP
Each bus in service shows up with its line number and color, and an arrow pointing where it heads. Filter by line to see only yours and its route.

🚌 FOLLOW A BUS
Tap a bus: the map follows it as it drives, and the list of its stops shows the live predicted times, how late or early it runs, and skipped stops.

🚏 YOUR STOPS
• Search by name, or the stops around you.
• The next buses at each stop, live when the bus is tracked, scheduled otherwise.
• Favorite stops with their next three buses.

📱 WIDGET
The next buses at your stop on the home screen, with a refresh button.

✨ ALSO
• Service alerts published by TCAT.
• Every line, Sunday and on-demand lines included.
• Light or dark theme in your wallpaper's colors.
• No account, no ads, no trackers.

Data: TCAT / Troyes Champagne Métropole on transport.data.gouv.fr (ODbL). Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
