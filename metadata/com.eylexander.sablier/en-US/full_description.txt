A daily limit isn't enough: you burn through it in one go in the morning. Sablier ("hourglass") limits your apps to a few minutes per hour. Enough to answer your messages, not to scroll forever.

⏳ TIME COMES BACK LIKE SAND
Every minute spent in an app comes back one period later: with "5 min per hour", a minute used at 2:10 pm is available again at 3:10 pm. No fixed reset time to game.

📱 YOUR RULES
• A group of apps sharing the same time (for example Instagram and its variants).
• 1 to 120 minutes, per 30 min, per hour, every 3 hours or per day.
• A timeline shows when you used them over the period.

🛑 THE PAUSE
A message warns you one minute before the end. Then the app is covered by a pause screen with a countdown until a minute is back. While paused, the rule can't be edited, switched off or deleted.

🔒 PRIVACY
Three permissions: usage access (to count the time and see which app opens), display over other apps (to cover a paused app with the pause screen) and notification access (to keep watching in the background, and set aside a paused app's notifications until the pause ends). No accessibility service, so your apps run at full speed. Sablier never reads screen or notification content. No account, no network, no ads: nothing leaves the phone.

The app's interface is in French.
