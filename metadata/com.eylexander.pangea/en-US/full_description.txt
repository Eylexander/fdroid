How well do you know the world? Pangea is a colorful geography game to test yourself and learn as you play, country after country.

🔮 MYSTERY COUNTRY
One outline a day, the same for everyone, and six guesses. Each guess tells you how far you are, in which direction, and how warm. Keep your streak going and share your result without spoiling it.

🚩 FLAGS, 🏛️ CAPITALS, 🧩 SHAPES
60-second rushes with four choices. Chain right answers for a combo up to x5; a miss costs three seconds. Wrong options come from the same region: telling Niger from Nigeria is the point. At the end, the countries to review, with their capital.

📍 PIN IT
Ten countries to place on a blank map. Spot on is 1000 points, then fewer the farther your pin. The map flies to the answer.

🧭 ATLAS
The world map to explore, with a card for every country: flag, capital, population, area. Countries you have found in any game light up in color.

🎯 LEARNS WITH YOU
Every game can be played on the whole world or one continent. Countries you miss come back more often until you get them right.

197 countries: the UN members plus Vatican, Palestine, Kosovo and Taiwan. In English or French, following the phone's language. Light and dark themes. No account, no network, no ads.
